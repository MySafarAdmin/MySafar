Upload students from a CSV file

One row per student, with the column names in the first row. Each student goes into the classroom of their Quran and Memorisation teacher, and classrooms that don't exist yet are created. Students are recognised by their Student ID, so uploading an updated list moves students with all of their records, updates changed details and never adds anyone twice. An optional House column (Makkah, Al-Aqsa or Madinah) sets each student's house. Your own column names are fine: the preview shows which column was taken for each detail and lets you change it.

The highlighted column is the class day you are recording. Tap a student's box and choose a score out of 5, or select the box and type 0 to 5. Tap the same score again to clear it.

The arrows move to the previous or next class day; on wider screens the days before and after stay in view on either side. The calendar goes to any day, including one outside the usual weekend classes.

A blank day means the score was not recorded, which is different from 0.
